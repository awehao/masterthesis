### Draft answer to the question
Without feeding in the actual table height, the fixed motion produces a complete 3D target in only 2 of the 6 layouts (0.60/P1, 0.45/P2).
- 0.45/P1: the bottle's lower part leaves the frame at the steep view → the view must change.
- 0.60/P2: the top cannot be confirmed (the outer edge lacks valid far background) → reject, or change view.
- 0.75: the camera is only 0.10 m above the table top; the silhouette and top cannot be confirmed, and the arm cannot reach higher → reject, or move the base / change the observation strategy.
- The base camera only supplies xy at every height.
- xy (known-radius fit) is about 0.6 mm in every layout, but this is the same bottle type, same scene, and the radius prior.
