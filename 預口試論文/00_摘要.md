# 摘要

> v0.8｜2026-11-24。與 `overleaf/abstract_zh.tex`、`abstract_en.tex` 同步，聚焦方法並補入住宅任務範圍；未新增成果數據。

本研究旨在建立一套適用於動態環境之移動機械臂預測導航與受約束操作系統，整合環境感知、幾何模型預測控制與底盤—手臂協調機制，以處理障礙避讓、末端追蹤及操作可達性等問題，並探討機器人在持續移動下執行操作任務之控制方法。

導航部分以平面剛體運動群 SE(2) 描述底盤位姿與追蹤誤差，透過有限時域預測規劃控制輸入，並結合障礙運動預測、控制屏障條件及原始雷射掃描防護，處理動態環境中的路徑追蹤與避障。

協同操作部分針對位置命令介面建立手臂執行模型，以關節位置與設定點構成增廣狀態，描述命令輸入至實際關節運動的追蹤動態。控制設計依據底盤與手臂對末端運動的共同影響，於預測時域內聯合最佳化控制輸入，並納入末端追蹤目標、關節限位與輪級運動限制。系統另以視覺量測建立接近與操作參考，依任務階段銜接導航與協同操作。

本研究以動態導航、抽屜操作及住宅場景整合任務進行模擬評估，整合開門通行、茶几圓柱之移動中抓取與車尾收納、開抽屜取筆及關閉抽屜、回程推門通行與關門，最後返回起點，以分析追蹤表現、任務銜接與執行效率。

**關鍵字：** 移動機械臂、幾何模型預測控制、全身協調控制、執行動態建模、控制屏障函數、移動中操作

# Abstract

This research aims to establish a predictive navigation and constrained manipulation system for mobile manipulators in dynamic environments. The system integrates environmental sensing, geometric model predictive control, and base–arm coordination to address obstacle avoidance, end-effector tracking, and manipulation reachability, with an emphasis on manipulation during motion.

For navigation, base poses and tracking errors are represented using SE(2). Control inputs are planned over a finite prediction horizon, combining obstacle motion prediction, control barrier conditions, and protection based on raw laser scans for path tracking and obstacle avoidance.

For coordinated manipulation, an arm execution model is developed for the position-command interface. An augmented state comprising joint positions and position setpoints describes tracking dynamics from commanded inputs to actual joint motion. Base and arm inputs are jointly optimized over a finite horizon according to their combined influence on end-effector motion, incorporating tracking objectives, joint limits, and wheel-level motion constraints. Visual measurements provide approach and manipulation references, while task phases connect navigation and coordinated manipulation.

Simulation evaluations cover dynamic navigation, drawer manipulation, and an integrated residential task. The task combines opening and traversing a doorway, picking up a tabletop cylinder during motion and placing it in a rear storage bin, opening a drawer to retrieve a pen and closing the drawer, pushing through and closing the door on the return trip, and returning to the starting point. Tracking performance, task transitions, and execution efficiency are evaluated.

**Keywords:** mobile manipulators, geometric model predictive control, whole-body coordination, execution dynamics modeling, control barrier functions, manipulation on the move
